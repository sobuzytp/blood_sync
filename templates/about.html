About BloodSync

BloodSync connects blood donors with people in urgent need of blood. Register as a donor, share your availability, and help save lives — or post a request when you or a loved one needs blood, and let the community respond.

This project was built as a Final Django Assignment, demonstrating Models, Views, Templates, Forms, CRUD operations, the Django ORM, authentication, template inheritance, validation, and search & filtering.
